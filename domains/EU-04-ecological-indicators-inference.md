# EU-04 — Ecological Indicators & Inference

This domain examines how measured variables and derived indicators support ecological interpretation. It distinguishes direct measurement, index construction, proxy relationships and modelled inference, with attention to validation and fitness for a stated purpose.

This foundational page identifies opportunities for research and resource development. The activities below are proposals, not a claim that completed modules already exist. Research, Methods & Tools, Education & Programmes and Applied Practice are all supported within this domain.

## Scope and principal subjects

- Variables, indices, proxies, contextual pressures and inferred properties
- Indicator selection, sensitivity, specificity and scale dependence
- Ground validation, uncertainty propagation and confounding
- Vegetation, soil, water, biodiversity and ecosystem-function indicators

## Relevant research questions

- What evidence supports the relationship between a proxy and the property of interest?
- How sensitive is an indicator to illumination, season, management or instrument choice?
- When does combining indicators improve interpretation, and when does it conceal uncertainty?

## Potential methods and how-to components

- Trace an indicator from source observation through processing to interpretation.
- Compare GRVI and ExG results under changing illumination.
- Design a validation plan for an indicator before using it in a decision.

## Teaching module opportunities

- Measurements, indicators and qualified inferences.
- Four Ways of Seeing Green: a proposed comparison of GRVI, ExG, VARI and GLI.

Teaching resources can be adapted to different pathways and difficulty levels. Each adaptation needs explicit prerequisites, learning objectives, tasks and expected outputs; the domain itself has no fixed difficulty.

## Applied research and design opportunities

- Select a defensible indicator set for an ecological intervention.
- Evaluate whether a proposed monitoring measure supports a management claim.
