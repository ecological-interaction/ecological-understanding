# EU-01 — Ecological Concepts & Theory

This domain examines the concepts, histories and theoretical positions through which ecological relationships become intelligible. It supports critical use of scientific models alongside reflection on the assumptions that guide observation, explanation and practice.

This foundational page identifies opportunities for research and resource development. The activities below are proposals, not a claim that completed modules already exist. Research, Methods & Tools, Education & Programmes and Applied Practice are all supported within this domain.

## Scope and principal subjects

- Ecological organisation, populations, communities and ecosystems
- Evolution, adaptation, niches, disturbance and succession
- Environmental philosophy, perception, ecological information and epistemology
- Scale, boundaries, uncertainty and competing explanations

## Relevant research questions

- How do different definitions of an ecosystem change the boundaries of an investigation?
- Which assumptions travel with a concept when it moves between scientific and design disciplines?
- How can contrasting theoretical accounts be evaluated without treating them as equivalent forms of evidence?

## Potential methods and how-to components

- Construct a concept map with definitions and attributed sources.
- Compare theoretical explanations of one observed ecological relationship.
- Prepare a glossary that records disagreement and changes in terminology.

## Teaching module opportunities

- Foundations of ecological reasoning: concepts, evidence and explanation.
- Histories of ecological information and environmental thought.

Teaching resources can be adapted to different pathways and difficulty levels. Each adaptation needs explicit prerequisites, learning objectives, tasks and expected outputs; the domain itself has no fixed difficulty.

## Applied research and design opportunities

- Use explicit ecological assumptions to frame a design brief.
- Compare how different theories influence a field research protocol.
