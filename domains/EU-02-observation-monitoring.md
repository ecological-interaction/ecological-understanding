# EU-02 — Observation & Monitoring

This domain investigates how ecological observations are produced, recorded and evaluated. It connects field methods, instruments and monitoring systems with sampling design, calibration and the limits of what an observation can establish.

This foundational page identifies opportunities for research and resource development. The activities below are proposals, not a claim that completed modules already exist. Research, Methods & Tools, Education & Programmes and Applied Practice are all supported within this domain.

## Scope and principal subjects

- Field observation, sampling strategies and repeat surveys
- Sensors, telemetry, physical computing and autonomous observation
- Laboratory and biological observations, including molecular sampling
- Calibration, ground truth, temporal coverage, provenance and uncertainty

## Relevant research questions

- Which sampling design can detect a specified change at a particular site?
- How do instrument placement and observation frequency shape what becomes visible?
- How should instrumental and human observations be compared while preserving their different methods and contexts?

## Potential methods and how-to components

- Design a monitoring question, sampling plan and field record.
- Document calibration and maintenance for an observation system.
- Compare repeat observations and identify missing data and bias.

## Teaching module opportunities

- From research question to observation protocol.
- Observation technologies across spatial and biological scales.

Teaching resources can be adapted to different pathways and difficulty levels. Each adaptation needs explicit prerequisites, learning objectives, tasks and expected outputs; the domain itself has no fixed difficulty.

## Applied research and design opportunities

- Design a place-specific monitoring plan for a restoration trial.
- Evaluate a sensor deployment against the needs of a land-management decision.
