# EU-09 — Ecological Design & Technology Choice

This domain examines how ecological understanding informs the selection and development of technologies and design practices. It treats technology choice as a contextual research problem involving ecological fit, material and energy flows, maintenance, social conditions and alternatives.

This foundational page identifies opportunities for research and resource development. The activities below are proposals, not a claim that completed modules already exist. Research, Methods & Tools, Education & Programmes and Applied Practice are all supported within this domain.

## Scope and principal subjects

- Place-specific design briefs, requirements and ecological constraints
- Technology comparison, suitability, resource flows and lifecycle reasoning
- Prototyping, fabrication, maintenance, repair and operational capacity
- Evaluation criteria, participatory design and practice-based research

## Relevant research questions

- Which ecological conditions should influence a technology choice at a particular site?
- How do maintenance capacity and material or energy requirements affect suitability?
- How can a design experiment generate research evidence as well as a working artefact?

## Potential methods and how-to components

- Translate a site assessment into explicit design requirements.
- Compare candidate technologies against evidence-based criteria.
- Prepare a prototype evaluation protocol including operational and ecological outcomes.

## Teaching module opportunities

- Ecological indicators informing technology choice.
- Design practice as a method of situated ecological inquiry.

Teaching resources can be adapted to different pathways and difficulty levels. Each adaptation needs explicit prerequisites, learning objectives, tasks and expected outputs; the domain itself has no fixed difficulty.

## Applied research and design opportunities

- Compare monitoring, water or agricultural technologies for a defined place.
- Develop a prototype and document how ecological evidence changes its design.
